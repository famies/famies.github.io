Talk to us about a location

What we do

1

Land banking advice

Buying several plots in a growth corridor and holding them is the simplest way to build land wealth, and the easiest way to lose money if the growth never arrives. We advise on where Enugu is actually building out, how to buy in bulk sensibly, and when to hold or sell.

2

Site-and-service plots

Undeveloped plots inside planned layouts, where roads and services are set out before building starts. We help you find them, understand what the layout provides, and go through the paperwork that comes with them.

3

Site inspection

Before any money moves, we visit the land, record its position and beacons, photograph and video it, and report back on what we found. If you are abroad, you see the ground as it is, not as it is described.

We look before you buy

Alongside the site visit, we screen every location we advise on against satellite imagery, recent building growth, and terrain data for erosion and flood exposure. It shows where a neighbourhood is genuinely filling in and where the ground itself is a risk, so our advice rests on more than word of mouth.

We share what that screen shows for your location in plain language, with the date of the imagery it came from.

What this is, and is not

  • • It tells you what the ground, the imagery and the public record show on a given date. It is not a guarantee about the land.
  • • It is not a survey, a valuation or a title search. Only a registered surveyor can survey land, and only an official search can tell you who holds the title.
  • • Before you pay anyone, get an official title search from the Enugu State Geographic Information System (ENGIS). We will help you request it.
